Kairon: Customer relationship intelligence

A retention platform in development that uses customer signals to support explainable risk scoring and follow-up workflows.

Project repository ↗

Problem

Kairon is designed to help customer-facing teams identify churn risk, understand the signals behind it, and plan retention decisions.

Users

The project README identifies customer success, account management, and revenue teams as its intended users.

Data / inputs
