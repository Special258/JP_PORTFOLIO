What this shows

Market signal extraction relies on mathematically transforming price series into normalized indicators that machine learning models and trading systems can process:

  • Moving Average Crossovers: Contrasts short-term momentum (20-day) against intermediate trends (50-day) to highlight structural direction.
  • Rolling Volatility: Standard deviation of daily logarithmic returns over a 20-day trailing window, measuring market regime shifts and dispersion.
  • Zero-Network Client Evaluation: All statistical calculations run entirely in the browser using lightweight math functions.

What it can't tell you

Quantitative features require careful interpretation and should never be viewed as standalone predictors:

  • Lagging Indicator Bias: Moving averages inherently lag real-time market shifts; crossovers occur only after price movement has begun.
  • Non-Stationarity: Financial market distributions evolve over time; past volatility regimes rarely repeat identically.
  • Execution Realities: The chart omits liquidity friction, bid-ask spreads, order book depth, and market impact.
