Hive: Community skill exchange

A non-monetary platform where people can share skills they know and find skills they want to learn.

Project repository ↗

Problem

Hive addresses the difficulty of finding a learning partner and gives people a way to share useful knowledge without making money the centre of the exchange.

Users

Community members who can each be both a teacher and a learner.

Data / inputs

Member profiles, skills they can teach, and skills they want to learn are used to discover potential learning partners.

Approach and architecture

The documented flow is: create a profile, list teachable and wanted skills, discover complementary members, then connect to arrange an exchange. The README describes a high-level web-app flow; its technology-stack table is marked as a placeholder.
